Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1 November 2026  ·  Corezyn Technologies

Corezyn Technologies ("we", "us", or "our") operates ZynBill, a GST billing and accounting software platform for Indian businesses. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal and business information when you use ZynBill (the "Service").

This policy is drafted in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of India. We are committed to handling your data with transparency and respect.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information when you register and use the Service:

  • Account & Identity: Full name, email address, mobile number, company name.
  • Business Identity: GSTIN (Goods and Services Tax Identification Number), business address, state of registration.
  • Device Information: Machine name and machine ID of each PC where the desktop application is activated, for licence enforcement purposes.
  • Usage & Authentication Logs: Login timestamps, login type (web portal or desktop application), IP address, browser/OS user-agent string, and login status (success or failure).
  • Business Data: Invoices, ledger entries, inventory records, customer and vendor details that you enter into the desktop application and that are synced to the server.

We do not collect payment card numbers, bank account details, Aadhaar numbers, or any other sensitive financial or government-issued identity numbers beyond your GSTIN.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your ZynBill account.
  • To verify your identity and enforce your subscription licence across the permitted number of PCs.
  • To deliver OTP-based authentication and security PINs for desktop login.
  • To display your business summary, invoice history, and reports in the customer portal.
  • To send transactional emails (OTPs, account alerts, renewal reminders).
  • To investigate suspicious login activity and maintain security of the platform.
  • To improve the Service based on aggregated, anonymised usage patterns.

3. Data Storage & Architecture

Your business data is stored in two places:

  • Local (your PC): The ZynBill desktop application stores all transaction data in an isolated SQLite database on your Windows machine. This data is under your direct physical control.
  • Server (our infrastructure): Selected data is synchronised to our server, stored in a tenant-isolated database. Each customer's data is stored in a separate database file and is never commingled with another customer's data.

Login activity logs (IP, timestamps, device identifiers) are stored server-side and retained for a maximum of 12 months for security audit purposes.

4. Data Retention

  • Account and business data is retained for the duration of your active subscription.
  • Upon account termination, you may request a full data export within 30 days. After 30 days, your data will be permanently and irreversibly deleted from our servers.
  • Login logs are automatically purged after 12 months.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or business data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Transactional email providers (for OTPs and alerts) operating under strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal Obligation: When required by a court order, government authority, or applicable Indian law.
  • Business Transfer: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred to the successor entity, subject to the same privacy protections.

6. Your Rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

As a Data Principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Lodge a complaint with our Data Protection Officer if you believe your rights have been violated.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate another individual to exercise your data rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, please write to us at privacy@corezyn.in. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit between the desktop application, portal, and server.
  • Tenant-isolated databases — no cross-customer data access is architecturally possible.
  • OTP-based login to prevent unauthorised portal access.
  • Security PIN requirement for desktop application sessions.
  • Login activity monitoring and anomaly logging.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security and disclaim liability for breaches outside our reasonable control.

8. Cookies

The ZynBill customer portal uses browser local storage (not third-party tracking cookies) to maintain your authenticated session. We do not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or any third-party pixel/tracking scripts on the portal.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended solely for use by businesses and their authorised representatives who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from persons under 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. We will notify you of material changes via email or a prominent notice on the portal at least 15 days before the changes take effect.

11. Contact & Grievance Officer

For privacy-related queries, data requests, or complaints:

  • Email: privacy@corezyn.in
  • Company: Corezyn Technologies

We aim to acknowledge all privacy requests within 72 hours and resolve them within 30 days.
