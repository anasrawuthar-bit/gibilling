Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: 1 November 2026  ·  Corezyn Technologies

This Refund and Cancellation Policy explains the terms under which payments for ZynBill subscriptions are handled by Corezyn Technologies. Please read this policy carefully before making a purchase.

1. Free Evaluation Period

All new ZynBill accounts are provided with a 30-day free evaluation period from the date of account activation. During this period:

  • No payment is required or collected.
  • You have full access to the desktop application and customer portal.
  • All data entered during evaluation is retained if you proceed to a paid subscription.
  • No credit card or payment details are required to begin the evaluation.

The evaluation period is designed to allow you to fully assess the software for your business needs before making any financial commitment.

2. Subscription Payment

At the end of the evaluation period, continued access to ZynBill requires the purchase of an annual subscription. Subscription fees are:

  • Charged in advance for the full annual term.
  • Non-refundable once the subscription has been activated and the licence key has been issued (see Section 3).
  • Subject to applicable GST as per Indian tax regulations. A GST invoice will be issued to your registered GSTIN upon payment.

3. No Refund Policy After Activation

Once a subscription is activated — meaning the annual licence has been issued and the software is accessible under the paid plan — no refunds will be issued for any reason, including but not limited to:

  • Change of mind or business circumstances.
  • Switching to a different software solution.
  • Partial use of the subscription period.
  • Technical issues that have been or can be resolved by our support team.
  • Downgrade to a lower-tier plan.

We strongly encourage all customers to take full advantage of the 30-day free evaluation period to verify that ZynBill meets their requirements before subscribing.

4. Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time through the customer portal or by contacting our support team. Upon cancellation:

  • Your subscription remains active until the end of the current paid annual term.
  • Auto-renewal will be disabled, and you will not be charged for the next cycle.
  • No prorated refund is issued for the unused portion of the current subscription term.
  • Access to the portal and cloud-sync features will cease at the end of the paid term.
  • The locally installed desktop application may continue to function in a read-only or offline mode after the subscription ends, as described in the Terms of Service.

To cancel, please contact us at support@corezyn.in at least 7 days before your renewal date.

5. Multi-PC Licensing

Subscriptions are priced based on the number of Windows PCs permitted to run the ZynBill desktop application simultaneously. If you need to reduce the number of licensed PCs:

  • Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date — no refund is issued for the current term.
  • Upgrades (adding more PCs) are prorated for the remainder of the current term and invoiced separately.

6. Exceptional Circumstances

Corezyn Technologies reserves the sole discretion to issue a full or partial refund in exceptional circumstances, such as:

  • A billing error or duplicate charge caused by our payment system.
  • A material failure of the Service that our support team is unable to resolve within a reasonable timeframe.

Refund requests under exceptional circumstances must be submitted within 7 days of the charge in question and will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis. Decisions are final.

7. Disputes

For any billing disputes or concerns, please contact our support team before initiating a chargeback with your payment provider. Chargebacks filed without prior contact with our team may result in immediate suspension of your account pending investigation.

8. Contact

For refund requests, billing queries, or cancellations, please reach out to us:

  • Email: support@corezyn.in
  • Company: Corezyn Technologies

We aim to respond to all billing queries within 2 business days.
